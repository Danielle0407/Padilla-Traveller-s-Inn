Our FAQ

Everything you need to know

Quick answers before you book.

How do I reserve a room?

Call or text 09759178563, or use the message tabs below to send a ready-made email or text. We’ll confirm availability for your dates.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 NN. Ask us if you need to arrive earlier or leave later.

How many rooms do you have?

We have exactly 4 cozy guest rooms for short-term and overnight stays.

Are meals available?

Yes. Delicious Lutong Bahay (home-cooked) meals are available upon request. Please tell us ahead of time.

Is the Wi-Fi free?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for all staying guests.

Is there parking for my vehicle?

Yes. We have a large, secure parking area for guest vehicles.
